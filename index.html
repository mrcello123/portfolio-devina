GET TO KNOW ME

About Me

I am driven by one question: what makes people care enough to act?

Before a single slide is drafted or designed, I put myself in the audience's seat, whether it's a client, partner, or customer.

Then I listen for the questions they'd never say out loud. Why should I care? Is this really worth my time? And what do I get here that I can't find anywhere else?

Most communication starts with what a brand wants to say. Mine starts with what people need to hear and works backward from there. That's how I approach marketing communications, PR, and every campaign or project I'm trusted with.
