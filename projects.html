Challenge & Strategy

Challenge

Designing a disability inclusion campus concert during COVID restrictions, requiring faculty approval and strict crowd control.

Strategy

Turned seating constraints (~65% reduction from 1,000 capacity) into an intimate concert concept where artists interacted directly with fans, pairing entertainment as the hook and inclusion as the core message.

Key Execution Highlights

  • Event Leadership: Pitched and secured faculty approval, managed minute-by-minute rundowns, benchmarked talent budgets, and led 60+ committee members across 7 divisions.
  • Pre-Event Campaign: Ran 5 Instagram Live sessions with disability advocates, school roadshows, and a themed band competition where contestants recorded inclusion songs.
  • Main Event & Social Impact: Hosted an inclusion talkshow with Johan Edward Tampubolon, SMKN 3 Bandung choir, student bands, Juicy Luicy headliner, and handed over Rp1,000,000 donations to Yayasan Rinjani on stage.
